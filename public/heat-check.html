Heat Check

Five coins you think are running hot. The board watches them every five minutes and yells when it's time to sell into the peak or drop the coin.

Warming up…

Type a ticker (SOL, HYPE, PEPE). Add the price you got in at if you want the board to also guard your entry: it says drop it at 8% under that price.

Riding Rising: up over the last 4 hours and above its 6-hour average.
Cooling Lost a step: slipped under its average or off today's high. Watch it.
Sell · peaking It ran up and is giving it back from the top. Take the win.
Drop it It's falling: a hard 4-hour slide, a bad day, or 8% under your entry.

Prices and hourly history come from CoinGecko, refreshed every five minutes while this page is open. These calls are simple rules on the last 24 hours of hourly prices, not advice. Your coins are saved in this browser. Memecoins can drop faster than any five-minute check.
