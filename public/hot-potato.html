Paper game, no real money. "Hot" means a Hyperliquid coin that is up on the day with at least $3M of volume (coins also trending on CoinGecko get picked first). Prices are Hyperliquid's live mid prices, streamed to this page as they change. Each buy and sell pays 0.5% to mimic swap costs. The chip on each card is a simple rule on the price since you caught it: it warns at −8% from your buy, or when a coin that ran up 8% gives back 4% from its top. Your game is saved in this browser.
